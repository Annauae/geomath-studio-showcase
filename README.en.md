# GeoMath Studio

[中文](README.md) | **English**

An interactive math canvas for learning and teaching: **dynamic geometry**, **function graphs**, and an **AI drawing assistant** in one place—so you can turn problems into diagrams faster and explain ideas more clearly.

- **Live demo**: `http://geomath.cn:18080/`

---

## What you can do

- **Dynamic geometry**: points, lines (segments/lines/rays), circles, intersections/midpoints, labels & measurements; drag and everything updates instantly
- **Function graphs**: explicit/implicit graphs; support “point sticks to curve/line/circle” style constraints
- **AI assistant**: describe a goal (text / screenshot scenarios) and get an executable sequence of drawing steps on the canvas

---

## Best for

- **Teaching demos**: show relationships change live while dragging
- **Practice & self-check**: build helper diagrams quickly and validate intuition
- **Explainer content**: interactive diagrams instead of static screenshots
